HOW TO UPLOAD

  Repository, Add file, Upload files. Select the eleven .html files AND the
  assets folder together and drag them in. Drag the assets FOLDER, not the
  two files inside it. Then commit.

AFTER THE COMMIT, CHECK THESE TWO URLS

     https://ceb1991.github.io/assets/style.css
     https://ceb1991.github.io/assets/enhance.js

  Both should show code. A 404 on either is what causes an unstyled header
  or a blank gap on the family page.

THE HEADER CONTROLS

  EN / ES switches language, the sun and moon switch changes the theme. Both
  are the same size, both remember your choice across pages, and both store
  it on your own device.
